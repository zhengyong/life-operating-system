**The case for Huang | The company · Technical barriers and strategic leadership**

### Introduction

NVIDIA itself may be the only company that can beat NVIDIA. Jensen Huang has been the person pushing it to keep surpassing its own achievements.

**One-sentence summary: Technology and ecosystem give NVIDIA its lead. Sustaining that lead requires sound strategic leadership.**

Here are five reasons.

### 1. Zeno’s paradox. Why are rivals always chasing yesterday’s NVIDIA?

From Hopper to Blackwell to Rubin, AMD has been working to catch up with NVIDIA’s previous generation while NVIDIA has expanded the race from GPUs to CPUs, networking, software and complete systems.

**Its competitors are pursuing a moving target.**

Zeno’s paradox offers no proof that a leader will stay ahead forever. It does capture how continuous iteration has helped NVIDIA preserve its advantage.

### 2. The Matthew effect. Why might a bigger lead produce an even stronger advantage?

More customers bring in more revenue, which pays for more R&D. More developers improve the CUDA ecosystem, which in turn draws in more customers.

The cycle reinforces itself.

AMD, Broadcom and chips designed in-house by cloud providers can make inroads in specific markets. **Catching up with a particular chip and replacing an entire AI computing platform are very different challenges.**

### 3. A lesson from history. Fuji did not bring down Kodak, and Motorola did not bring down Nokia

At the very least, traditional competitors alone did not reshape these industries.

Kodak failed to make a successful commercial transition to digital imaging. Nokia lost its dominance in the contest over smartphone operating systems and app ecosystems.

**The greatest danger for an industry leader may come from a new set of rules, even if the runner-up never catches it.**

NVIDIA also needs to guard against becoming the next Kodak or Nokia.

### 4. Huang’s rarest ability is his willingness to retire yesterday’s NVIDIA

From gaming graphics cards to CUDA, and from AI training to data centers, inference, agents and physical AI, Huang has repeatedly pushed NVIDIA into a new technology cycle.

**A strong CEO can run today’s company. An exceptional founder may also keep redefining what that company can become.**

Huang’s greatest contribution is his ability to sustain investment in technology and keep the company reinventing itself.

### 5. What does NVIDIA gain by replacing Huang?

The departure of one CEO will not immediately cost NVIDIA its products, customers or engineering teams.

Still, the AI industry will change substantially over the next decade.

**The biggest succession question is whether NVIDIA can make equally sound strategic decisions during the next technological transition. Tomorrow’s GPU sales tell us little about that.**

### Summary

**NVIDIA’s moat comes from more than three decades of technical work. Huang’s contribution is his ability to keep extending it into new territory.**

NVIDIA will remain a formidable company without him.

**Will it still be the NVIDIA we know today?**

I have my doubts.

**For readers to consider. If another computing revolution arrives, who will lead NVIDIA into the next generation of industry leadership?**
