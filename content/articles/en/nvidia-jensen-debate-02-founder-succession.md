**The case for Huang | The entrepreneur · The scarcity of exceptional leadership**

### Introduction

Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Dario Amodei and Tim Cook, who spent years running Apple, are all prominent technology leaders.

If asked to take over NVIDIA today, which of them would want the job? Who could do it well from day one?

**One-sentence summary: There are plenty of capable CEOs. What is hard to reproduce is the breadth of leadership Huang and NVIDIA developed together over more than three decades.**

I want to make five points.

### 1. Exceptional entrepreneurs are scarce

The world has many professional managers. Few can lead a major technology company for years and steer it successfully through multiple industry cycles.

Musk has his space and automotive businesses. Altman and Amodei have AI companies of their own.

**A top entrepreneur is not someone a company can hire or replace on demand.**

Even someone NVIDIA could recruit might be a poor fit.

### 2. What does an exceptional CEO need to do well?

Strategy, technology, organizational management, commercial operations and coordination across an industry.

It is easy to imagine someone excelling in any one of those areas.

**The hard part is being able to handle all five over time and repeatedly make major decisions that work out.**

In more than three decades, Huang has shown that he can turn technical innovation into a business that keeps developing.

### 3. Huang’s management style is hard to copy

Huang has said publicly that he had around 60 executives reporting directly to him.

He emphasizes fewer layers of management, shared information and direct participation by different teams in solving problems.

**Anyone can copy an organization chart with 60 direct reports. Running an organization that way takes another set of skills.**

The approach depends on a CEO’s deep knowledge of the technology, the business and the people.

### 4. If NVIDIA looks outside, who can take over immediately?

A CEO who needs to learn CUDA, GPU architecture, the global supply chain, customer relationships and the next generation of AI technology will face a much bigger task than simply taking over an office.

**Even entrepreneurs who were extraordinarily successful elsewhere may take time to become effective at NVIDIA.**

Why assume that a professional manager with an impressive résumé could take over easily?

### 5. Is an internal hire necessarily better?

NVIDIA’s own executives know the company and may have deep technical and managerial experience.

Running a division and setting the direction of an entire AI computing platform pose different challenges.

**Over many years, Huang has shaped the way NVIDIA organizes itself, chooses its technical direction and works as a company.**

Filling the position is easier than taking on the full range of responsibilities and influence he brought to it.

### Summary

**Excellent entrepreneurs exist. Excellence alone establishes neither a fit with NVIDIA nor readiness to take over immediately.**

Huang and NVIDIA grew together for more than three decades.

What makes Huang unusual is how closely his experience fits NVIDIA’s history, technology and organization. That does not require him to be better than every other CEO.

**NVIDIA can appoint a new CEO. Finding another Jensen Huang is a much harder proposition.**

**For readers to consider. If you were choosing NVIDIA’s next CEO, who would give you more confidence?**
