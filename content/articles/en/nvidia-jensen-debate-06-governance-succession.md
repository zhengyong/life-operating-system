**The case for succession | The entrepreneur · Organizational capability and corporate governance**

### Introduction

Some argue that even Musk, Altman or Amodei might have trouble taking over NVIDIA. How could another CEO possibly do it?

**I think that starts with the wrong question. Why does NVIDIA have to find another Jensen Huang?**

**One-sentence summary: The CEO’s job is to lead the existing organization through its next stage of development.**

I want to make five points.

### 1. Great founders are scarce. Capable successors still exist

Leaders such as Huang and Musk have different experiences and abilities.

Successful companies are led by more than one kind of CEO.

Some create new markets, some expand mature businesses, some bring organizations together, and some excel at running complex global operations.

**NVIDIA needs a leader suited to its next phase.**

### 2. Huang’s success also testifies to the strength of NVIDIA’s people

The CEO of a global technology company cannot design every chip, write CUDA, build networks and coordinate the supply chain alone.

NVIDIA’s achievements come from the engineers, researchers and managers it has built up over time.

**Giving Huang all the credit would underrate the organization he created.**

One of the great achievements of an entrepreneur is developing teams that can take responsibility on their own.

### 3. Sixty direct reports do not make succession impossible

Huang adopted a very flat management structure and has said publicly that he had around 60 executives reporting to him.

That also means that many senior managers have long participated in major decisions and coordination across divisions.

**A team that understands the technology, customers and company culture provides a foundation for continuity.**

The next CEO can choose a management style appropriate to the job ahead.

### 4. The CEO is a governance role a company must be able to fill

A company that only one person can lead may have a key-person risk.

Boards, executive teams, internal controls and strategic planning exist to keep a company working as its leadership changes.

In November 2026, NVIDIA appointed longtime CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and began looking at internal and external candidates for the permanent role.

**The transition is an example of public-company governance doing its job. Huang’s achievements stand on their own.**

### 5. The successor’s job is to make NVIDIA succeed in its next phase

The successor does not need to reinvent CUDA or build global customer relationships from scratch.

The immediate tasks are to keep the technical roadmap on track, sustain R&D investment, strengthen collaboration and make new strategic choices when needed.

**Succession tests whether the organization can keep creating value.**

### Summary

Huang can be an exceptional founder and NVIDIA can still be led by someone else.

**A mature company can thrive without requiring every CEO to become a legend.**

It needs a clear strategy, reliable institutions, strong teams and a leader who can keep creating value.

**For readers to consider. Is a great company’s highest achievement having an irreplaceable founder, or becoming strong enough to stand on its own?**
