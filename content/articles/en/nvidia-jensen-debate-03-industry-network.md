**The case for Huang | The industry · The global AI ecosystem and strategic relationships**

### Introduction

Among NVIDIA’s greatest assets, alongside its GPUs, CUDA and engineering teams, is the global network of industry partnerships that Huang built over more than three decades.

**One-sentence summary: Orders can be handed over and contracts can continue. Trust and the ability to coordinate strategy across companies are much harder to pass on.**

There are five parts to that argument.

### 1. TSMC. Can money alone buy the most advanced capacity?

NVIDIA designs excellent chips. It still relies on partners for advanced manufacturing and packaging.

Through years of work together, NVIDIA and TSMC have built a deep relationship in advanced process technology, packaging and capacity planning.

**Design determines how powerful an AI chip can be. Supply-chain coordination helps determine whether it reaches customers on time.**

That relationship goes well beyond a contract signed by procurement.

### 2. Microsoft and Amazon. Why do customers work with a competitor?

Technology companies such as Microsoft and Amazon develop their own AI chips while continuing to adopt NVIDIA’s computing platform.

Amazon Web Services, for example, has announced plans to deploy millions of additional NVIDIA GPUs.

**How do you persuade major customers developing their own alternatives to keep buying your next generation of products?**

It takes a technical edge, a credible long-term roadmap and the ability to work with them commercially.

### 3. OpenAI and other AI companies. From selling chips to planning the future together

NVIDIA and OpenAI previously announced a plan for 10 gigawatts of AI infrastructure.

The work goes beyond exchanging a price quote and a purchase order.

It requires long-term coordination around future models, computing architecture, data centers and delivery schedules.

**A partnership becomes a strategic asset when a customer’s future development is closely tied to its supplier’s next product generation.**

### 4. Huang’s real network spans the global AI industry

TSMC manufactures chips. Cloud providers build computing infrastructure. Companies such as OpenAI develop models. Server, networking and energy companies support deployment.

Each has its own objectives and interests.

**One of Huang’s strengths is keeping these companies working together around NVIDIA’s platform.**

The achievement lies in giving different companies confidence that working with NVIDIA can create lasting value for them.

### 5. Can a successor inherit this network intact?

Customers will not stop buying GPUs the moment Huang leaves.

Contracts will remain in effect.

Can the same strategic trust, ability to coordinate major projects and personal influence at crucial moments pass to a successor intact?

**Products and contracts can be handed over. Trust built between people over decades rarely transfers overnight.**

### Summary

**NVIDIA is a chip company and an important platform connecting the global AI industry.**

Huang shaped its products and technology and spent years building its global network of partners.

**Replacing him may cost NVIDIA some of its hard-to-measure ability to coordinate the industry, even if it keeps its customers.**

**For readers to consider. If Huang’s network is a significant intangible asset for NVIDIA, how should we value it?**
