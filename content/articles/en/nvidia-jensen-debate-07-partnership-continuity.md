**The case for succession | The industry · Commercial interests and the global partnership ecosystem**

### Introduction

TSMC, Microsoft, Amazon and OpenAI are among Huang’s many industry relationships.

Do they work with NVIDIA because of Huang personally, or because NVIDIA can create value for them?

**One-sentence summary: Commercial partnerships rest on technology, shared interests and long-term contracts. Huang’s personal relationships are one important part of that foundation.**

I want to make five points.

### 1. TSMC. Commercial value sustains a long-term relationship

NVIDIA needs TSMC’s advanced manufacturing capability. TSMC benefits from NVIDIA’s large demand for chips.

Their work together covers process technology, packaging, capacity planning and long-term roadmaps.

**Changing the CEO is unlikely to make TSMC suddenly stop supplying chips to NVIDIA.**

The main factor in whether the partnership continues is whether the companies keep creating value together.

### 2. Microsoft and Amazon. They buy GPUs because they need the computing power

Cloud giants have procurement teams, technical evaluation processes and long-term investment plans of their own.

They buy NVIDIA computing platforms for AI training, inference and cloud services.

**Infrastructure investments in the tens of billions of dollars take more than a personal relationship between CEOs.**

As long as NVIDIA supplies competitive products and delivers reliably, customers have good commercial reasons to keep working with it.

### 3. OpenAI and other AI companies. Strategic partnerships belong to the companies

NVIDIA’s work with companies such as OpenAI involves technical architecture, computing requirements, long-term planning and collaboration between organizations.

Engineers, researchers and business teams at both companies do much of the work.

**Agreement at the top has developed into concrete technical projects and long-term commitments between companies.**

Leaders can get a partnership moving. Day-to-day work depends on the teams carrying it out.

### 4. An industry ecosystem can outlast a personal network

NVIDIA’s ecosystem includes developers, system manufacturers, cloud platforms and software businesses worldwide.

CUDA will still have developers after Huang leaves. NVLink will still do the same technical work under a new CEO.

**The strength of an ecosystem is its ability to run on shared interests without having to rely on one person’s coordination.**

If every partnership required Huang’s involvement, the ecosystem would have more maturing to do.

### 5. New leadership can change how partnerships work without weakening them

A new CEO may communicate differently and take a more formal approach to customer relationships, commitments to partners and supply-chain coordination.

A decline in one person’s influence need not reduce the company’s commercial influence.

Product performance, technology decisions, delivery and customer value still determine NVIDIA’s position.

**Partners need a company that consistently keeps its promises. The signature can change.**

### Summary

The relationships Huang built deserve respect.

**His network accounts for only part of NVIDIA’s customer relationships and his personal influence for only part of its competitiveness.**

A mature industry ecosystem should be capable of developing beyond its founder’s tenure.

**My judgment is that NVIDIA’s global network has strong reasons to keep working as long as the technology and business value are there.**

**For readers to consider. If Microsoft, Amazon and TSMC still need NVIDIA, would they stop working with it just because Huang leaves?**
