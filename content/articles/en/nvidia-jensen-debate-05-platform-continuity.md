**The case for succession | The company · Technical barriers and strategic continuity**

### Introduction

Will NVIDIA lose its competitive edge when Jensen Huang leaves?

**I see it differently. NVIDIA’s greatest strength is that it has built an AI computing system that can keep operating without depending on one person.**

**One-sentence summary: Huang created NVIDIA’s competitive lead, and those advantages have become durable assets of the company.**

I want to make five points.

### 1. Zeno’s paradox. Rivals are chasing NVIDIA as a company

From Hopper to Blackwell to Rubin, what drives NVIDIA’s product iterations is a large engineering workforce, an established research operation and a product roadmap.

**CUDA and the research teams will still be there after Huang leaves. Work on the next generation will carry on.**

NVIDIA has already turned its founder’s strategic judgments into capabilities the organization can use.

### 2. The Matthew effect. NVIDIA’s lead can outlast a change of CEO

Customers, revenue, R&D spending, developers and supply-chain partnerships have come to reinforce one another.

**Once a real moat is in place, its survival is no longer wholly up to the person who dug it.**

AMD, Broadcom and in-house chips from cloud providers still face NVIDIA’s entire computing platform.

### 3. Kodak and Nokia offer another lesson

Kodak and Nokia show that a company cannot live forever on the business practices that made it successful.

Their experience does not show that only a founder can lead the next round of innovation.

**A company needs ways to correct mistakes, change itself and adapt as technology advances.**

A culture that only one person can sustain already carries a long-term risk.

### 4. Why might near-term operations avoid serious disruption?

On November 17, 2026, NVIDIA reported roughly $115.8 billion in third-quarter revenue, up 103% year over year. Its revenue outlook for the following quarter was about $130 billion.

Those results mainly reflect what the company did before the leadership transition. They do not prove the transition will have no effect.

They do show that **NVIDIA has a strong base of orders, a mature roadmap and a computing platform it is actively delivering.**

Changing the CEO will not automatically bring near-term operations to a halt.

### 5. Long-term growth depends on making innovation part of the organization

Demand for AI training, inference, agents and physical AI will continue after Huang leaves.

NVIDIA’s ability to stay ahead depends on research, product delivery, value to customers and how well its teams execute.

**A great technology company should be able to make its founder’s strategic thinking part of the way it operates for the long run.**

### Summary

Huang’s historical contribution is beyond dispute.

**Today’s NVIDIA is a global AI platform built by its people, technology, customers, capital and ecosystem, with achievements that extend beyond its founder.**

**My judgment is that NVIDIA can keep its business running in the near term and continue along its established strategy.**

**For readers to consider. If the moat is deep enough, why assume the same CEO has to stay in place for it to survive?**
